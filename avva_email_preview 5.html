Business Development Representative (Creator Partnerships)

Be part of a creator-driven growth effort that sources and qualifies high-impact creators for our affiliate program, owning the top of funnel to deliver fully documented, ready-to-activate affiliates to the Affiliate Management team.

This role delivers measurable impact by rapidly identifying creators across social platforms, vetting fit and brand-safety, executing structured outreach cadences, and preparing precise handoffs so Affiliate Managers can activate partners efficiently. You will own outreach and qualification through the moment of handoff and stay accountable until the Affiliate Manager confirms first contact.

Responsibilities:

Sourcing & Prospecting
- Identify creators and influencers across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, blogs, podcasts, and newsletters using hashtag/keyword search, competitor rosters, organic mentions, and partner referrals. Confirm prospect activity (e.g., posted within the last 30 days) and document source, platform, handle, and a one-line fit note before outreach.

Qualification & Vetting
- Screen prospects against approved criteria: audience alignment, minimum reach threshold, content quality, absence of undisclosed sponsorships or FTC violations, and no exclusive ties to direct competitors. Flag and escalate brand-safety or engagement-quality concerns per protocol. Confirm verbal or written commitment and ensure the intake form is complete prior to handoff.

Outreach & Relationship Handoffs
- Execute the structured outreach cadence (multi-touch across email and DMs) using approved scripts and talking points. Handle common objections using approved responses and route cold leads into automated re-engagement when appropriate. Prepare and submit a complete handoff package and introduce the assigned Affiliate Manager by name, setting expectations for next contact.

Documentation & Collaboration
- Maintain accurate CRM records for every prospect, touch, and outcome. Prepare comprehensive handoff summaries documenting recruitment context, commitment details, and relationship notes. Work closely with Affiliate Managers, leadership, and fellow BDRs to keep the pipeline clean and consistent in brand voice.

Benefits:
- Join a momentum-driven affiliate program with clear targets, a defined handoff process, and a collaborative team that supports activation and follow-through.

Mandatory Requirements:

Experience & Role Fit
- 1–2 years in business development, sales development, outbound prospecting, affiliate/influencer recruiting, or a similar client-facing sales-support role.

Skills & Tools
- Strong verbal and written communication; ability to craft human-sounding outreach. High attention to detail and rigorous CRM hygiene. Proficiency with CRM tools (HubSpot or similar) and Microsoft Office/Google Workspace.

Work Style & Availability
- Comfortable with high-volume outbound work (cold email, DMs, calls) in a fast-paced, target-driven environment. Reliable attendance and punctuality.

Education
- High school diploma or equivalent required.

Desirable Requirements:
- Associate's or Bachelor's degree preferred. Experience in affiliate marketing, influencer partnerships, or the creator economy. Familiarity with FTC disclosure requirements and compliance red flags. Bilingual abilities (e.g., Spanish/English) and experience with call/outreach monitoring or QA environments are a plus.

Hard Data:
- Outreach cadence: 5–7 touches across email and DM over approximately two weeks.
- CRM: HubSpot (or similar) required for accurate tracking and handoffs.
- SLAs: Respond to inbound interest within 1 hour; make first contact on new prospects within 15 minutes of qualification; maintain identification-to-first-outreach under 24 hours. Expect Affiliate Manager to make contact within 5 business days after handoff.

Success Metrics & Interactions:
- Daily and weekly benchmarks for new qualified prospects, outbound emails, calls, and touches; discovery calls booked; affiliate presentations delivered. Maintain fast qualification speed and adhere to team SLAs for response and follow-up.

About the Project:
- Work within a consumer-focused marketplace built on trust and community, recruiting creators who align with a non-toxic, family-safety-oriented brand voice. This program emphasizes creator-driven word-of-mouth growth and scalable affiliate activation while maintaining strict brand-safety standards.
